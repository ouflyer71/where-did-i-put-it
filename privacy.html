🔒 Privacy · Where Did I Put It

Short version: your stuff stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

No accounts

There is nothing to sign up for and no login. The app doesn't know who you are.

No tracking or analytics

The app has no analytics, ads, trackers or cookies. It doesn't contact any other company's servers. It only loads its own files from the site that hosts it, GitHub Pages (ouflyer71.github.io). Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses.

Your items stay on your device

Everything you save is stored only in this browser on this device: item names, spots, tags, photos and loans. Searching, "Where's my…?" answers and the spoken replies (your phone's built-in voice) all happen on the device. Nothing is uploaded.

Voice input

The 🎤 buttons use your phone's own speech recognition. The app only receives the finished text, never the audio.

  • iPhone (Safari): Safari uses Apple's speech recognition, the same system as Siri and Dictation. Depending on your iPhone model, language and settings, Apple may process the audio on the device or send it to Apple's servers. Apple's privacy policy covers that.
  • iPhone Home Screen app: iOS doesn't let Home Screen web apps use in-app speech recognition, so the app points you to the 🎤 on your keyboard instead (Apple Dictation).
  • Android / Chrome: speech is usually processed by Google's speech service under Google's privacy policy.

If you'd rather not use voice at all, just type.

Backups are files you control

💾 Back up saves a file that you choose where to keep, for example in Files or iCloud Drive. The app never sends it anywhere.

Feedback

Feedback is only sent if you choose to send it. It goes through your own email app or share sheet, and you see everything before it goes. It never includes your items, spots or photos. "Include app version & device info" adds the app version, browser/device type, how many items you have and whether the app is installed.

Deleting your data

You can delete items in the app. Clearing this site's data in your browser settings, or deleting the Home Screen app, erases everything on that device. Back up first if you want to keep it.
